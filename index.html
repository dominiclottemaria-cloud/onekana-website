01 — WHY WE EXIST

EXTRAORDINARY TALENT SHOULD NOT REMAIN UNSEEN.

Many emerging East African athletes stay overlooked despite exceptional dedication and potential. Existing systems only recognise athletes after success — leaving countless stories, communities and journeys unseen.

East African running is deeper than medals and podiums. Behind every champion are communities, cultures, traditions and untold stories that shape one of the world’s strongest running ecosystems.

If ONEKANA succeeds, visibility becomes more accessible — athletes are introduced earlier, communities become part of the narrative, and opportunity reaches talent first.
